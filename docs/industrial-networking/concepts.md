# Industrial Communication Architecture

This section covers the strategic approach to documenting real-time industrial communication systems.

## Key Principles

1.  **Protocol-Oriented Documentation:** Highlighting the specific data models and communication patterns of Modbus and OPC architectures.
2.  **Addressing & Mapping:** Clear visualization of register maps, node IDs, and data types for engineers and integrators.
3.  **Real-Time Constraints:** Documenting latency, polling frequencies, and heartbeat mechanisms critical for industrial reliability.

### Case Study: OPC Unified Architecture (UA)
Documenting the transition from legacy OPC DA to the secure, platform-independent OPC UA, focusing on information modeling and security configurations.
